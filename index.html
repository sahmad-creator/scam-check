Fake job message checker

Paste a job offer from WhatsApp, Telegram or email. You get a risk score and the exact reasons. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

This is a rule-based checker, not a verdict. A low score does not prove a job is real, and a high score is a reason to stop and verify, not proof of fraud. Never pay a fee, share an OTP, or send Aadhaar/PAN/bank details to get a job. Verify the company on its official website and report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
